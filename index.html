About

Research depth with an applied consultancy mindset.

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Technological University Dublin and a former lecturer at Makerere University. My work spans efficient AI, natural-language processing, digital health, bioacoustics, and industry-facing technical feasibility studies.

I have led and contributed to multidisciplinary projects involving research design, requirements analysis, prototyping, model evaluation, data engineering, technical reporting, stakeholder engagement, and implementation planning.
